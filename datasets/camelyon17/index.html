Dataset

CAMELYON17

Cancer Metastases in Lymph Nodes Challenge 2017

A five-centre extension of CAMELYON16 that moves from single slides to patients: slides are grouped into artificial patients and scored by pN stage. Useful for testing robustness across centres and scanners.

Official entry

registry.opendata.aws/camelyon

Primary paper

Litjens et al. 1399 H&E-stained sentinel lymph node sections of breast cancer patients: the CAMELYON dataset. GigaScience 7(6), giy065 (2018)

Version & evaluation

Version
CAMELYON17 portion of the combined CAMELYON release (GigaScience 2018).[camelyon-gigascience]
Official splits
500 training slides / 100 training patients with labels; 500 test slides / 100 test patients released without the reference standard.[camelyon-gigascience]
Evaluation protocol
Patient-level pN stage, scored with a weighted Cohen's kappa.[camelyon-gigascience]

Access & terms

How you get the data and what you may do with it are recorded separately.

How to obtain
Open download. Same public S3 bucket as CAMELYON16 on the Registry of Open Data on AWS.[aws-registry]
Licence / terms
CC0[camelyon-gigascience]
Redistribution
Public-domain dedication; no restriction on redistribution stated.[camelyon-gigascience]

Known limitations

  • Detailed metastasis contours exist for only 50 of the 1,000 slides (10 per centre); the rest have slide-level labels.[camelyon-gigascience]
  • Test-set labels were withheld for challenge evaluation, so local test numbers are not comparable to leaderboard results.[camelyon-gigascience]
