Dataset

PANDA

Prostate cANcer graDe Assessment · PANDA challenge

A large public set of prostate biopsy WSIs with grade labels, released for a 2020 Kaggle competition that the paper describes as the largest histopathology competition to date. Each biopsy has an ISUP grade group and Gleason score; many training slides also carry partial masks whose label scheme differs by contributing centre.

Official entry

panda.grand-challenge.org/data

Primary paper

Bulten et al. Artificial intelligence for diagnosis and Gleason grading of prostate cancer: the PANDA challenge. Nat Med 28, 154-163 (2022)

Version & evaluation

Version
Kaggle competition release (2020). Only the development set is public.[panda-paper]Tuning (393), internal validation (545) and external validation (741 US + 330 EU) biopsies are not part of the public download.
Official splits
No official train/validation split of the public data. Competition scoring used hidden tuning and internal-validation sets.[panda-paper]
Evaluation protocol
ISUP grade group per biopsy, scored with quadratically weighted Cohen's kappa against the reference standard.[panda-paper]

Access & terms

How you get the data and what you may do with it are recorded separately.

How to obtain
Platform account + rules. Download from Kaggle after signing in and accepting the competition rules.[kaggle-data]
Licence / terms
CC BY-SA-NC 4.0 (as stated by the organisers)[panda-paper]The paper and the Kaggle competition rules state the same licence.
Redistribution
Non-commercial research use with attribution (cite the PANDA paper); share-alike. The Kaggle page lists the data as "Subject to Competition Rules", and those rules grant CC BY-SA-NC 4.0 for competition, academic and non-commercial use.[panda-paper]

Known limitations

  • Mask values mean different things per provider (Radboud labels glands by Gleason pattern; Karolinska labels benign vs cancerous regions). Not every training slide has a mask, and masks may contain errors.[kaggle-data]
  • Some training slides contain pen marks; test slides do not.[kaggle-data]
  • Training labels were typically set by a single pathologist. All private test images and most public test images were graded by multiple pathologists.[kaggle-data]The single-reader wording is from the PANDA paper; the test-set wording is from Kaggle.
  • Focuses on acinar adenocarcinoma at the biopsy level, not patient-level diagnosis across multiple cores.[panda-paper]
