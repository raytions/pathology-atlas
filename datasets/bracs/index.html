Dataset

BRACS

BReAst Carcinoma Subtyping

Breast lesion WSIs and extracted regions labelled by consensus of three pathologists across benign, atypical and malignant categories. Ships with patient-level train / validation / test splits at both slide and region level.

Official entry

www.bracs.icar.cnr.it

Primary paper

Brancati et al. BRACS: A Dataset for BReAst Carcinoma Subtyping in H&E Histology Images. arXiv 2111.04740 (2021)

Version & evaluation

Version
Release described in arXiv 2111.04740 v1. Earlier subsets used in prior publications are kept in a "previous_versions" archive.[bracs-preprint]
Official splits
Predefined patient-level splits. WSI level, 395 train / 67 validation / 85 test slides (133 / 25 / 31 patients).[bracs-preprint]
Evaluation protocol
Three lesion types or seven subtypes; the paper does not fix a single metric.[bracs-preprint]

Access & terms

How you get the data and what you may do with it are recorded separately.

How to obtain
Registration required. Register on the BRACS website and accept the terms of use, then download from its FTP server.[bracs-preprint]
Licence / terms
CC0 (as stated in the dataset paper)[bracs-preprint]
Redistribution
Not restricted by CC0; registration is still required to download.[bracs-preprint]

Known limitations

  • Single institution and a single scanner (Aperio AT2, 0.25 µm/pixel at 40x), so scanner and site shift are not represented.[bracs-preprint]
  • The current terms on the BRACS website could not be read (the site renders with JavaScript); the licence shown here comes from the paper.[bracs-site]
