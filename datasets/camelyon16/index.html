Dataset

CAMELYON16

Cancer Metastases in Lymph Nodes Challenge 2016

Sentinel lymph node WSIs from two Dutch hospitals with exhaustive metastasis contours, built for the first WSI challenge in computational pathology. Small, well annotated and still the default sanity check for slide-level metastasis detection.

Official entry

registry.opendata.aws/camelyon

Primary paper

Ehteshami Bejnordi et al. Diagnostic assessment of deep learning algorithms for detection of lymph node metastases in women with breast cancer. JAMA 318, 2199-2210 (2017)

Version & evaluation

Version
CAMELYON16 portion of the combined CAMELYON release (GigaScience 2018).[camelyon-gigascience]
Official splits
Official split of 270 training slides (110 with metastases, 160 without) and 129 test slides (49 with, 80 without).[c16-jama]
Evaluation protocol
Two challenge tasks, lesion-level detection (FROC) and slide-level classification (AUC).[c16-jama]

Access & terms

How you get the data and what you may do with it are recorded separately.

How to obtain
Open download. Hosted in the public "camelyon-dataset" S3 bucket on the Registry of Open Data on AWS, together with CAMELYON17.[aws-registry]
Licence / terms
CC0[camelyon-gigascience]
Redistribution
Public-domain dedication; no restriction on redistribution stated.[camelyon-gigascience]

Known limitations

  • Only two centres; slides containing only isolated tumour cells were excluded.[camelyon-gigascience]
  • The organisers flag 15 slides that may contain unlabelled metastatic areas (listed in a text file in the release).[camelyon-gigascience]
