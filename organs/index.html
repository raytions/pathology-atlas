Task families

Organ-independent task types. A method built for a family is "applicable" to its clinical tasks, which is different from having been evaluated on them.

  • ROI / patch classification

    Classifying fixed regions cut from slides rather than whole slides. Common as a linear-probe benchmark for pretrained encoders, and as the region-level track of datasets such as BRACS.

  • Self-supervised representation learning

    Pretraining an image encoder on unlabelled histology so its patch features can feed many downstream tasks. Encoders are judged by how well light-weight heads perform on top of them.

  • Weakly supervised slide classification

    The task family behind most slide-level benchmarks: a gigapixel slide is tiled into patches, patch features are aggregated, and only the slide label supervises training. Grading, metastasis detection and subtyping are all instances of it.
