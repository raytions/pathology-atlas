Tasks

Datasets and methods are linked through tasks. Clinical tasks belong to an organ; task families are organ-independent types such as slide classification. A method counts as evaluated only when a source reports a result for that task.

Task families

A method built for a family applies to its clinical tasks, which is different from having been evaluated on them.

  • ROI / patch classification

    Classifying fixed regions cut from slides rather than whole slides. Common as a linear-probe benchmark for pretrained encoders, and as the region-level track of datasets such as BRACS.

    3 clinical tasks · 7 methods

  • Self-supervised representation learning

    Pretraining an image encoder on unlabelled histology so its patch features can feed many downstream tasks. Encoders are judged by how well light-weight heads perform on top of them.

    0 clinical tasks · 9 methods

  • Weakly supervised slide classification

    The task family behind most slide-level benchmarks: a gigapixel slide is tiled into patches, patch features are aggregated, and only the slide label supervises training. Metastasis detection and cancer subtyping, the applications in the cited paper, are instances of it.

    4 clinical tasks · 13 methods
