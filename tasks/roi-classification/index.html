Task · Task family

ROI / patch classification

Classifying fixed regions cut from slides rather than whole slides. Common as a linear-probe benchmark for pretrained encoders, and as the region-level track of datasets such as BRACS.[uni-paper]

Prediction target
One label per region of interest or tile.
Clinical tasks
Breast lesion subtyping, Colorectal tissue classification, Gleason pattern classification
