Task · Clinical task

Gleason pattern classification

Labelling small prostate tissue patches by their Gleason pattern rather than grading a whole biopsy. It is a region-level task with its own label set and metrics, so results on it are not results on biopsy-level Gleason grading.[sicap-preprint]

Prediction target
Gleason pattern of a tissue patch (non-cancerous, 3, 4 or 5); in SICAPv2 the majority grade of the patch mask.
Organ
Prostate
Disease
Prostate adenocarcinoma
Task family

    Dataset

    Prostate core-needle biopsy patches (512 x 512 at 10x) from 155 slides of 95 patients, each with a pixel-level Gleason grade mask, plus a primary and secondary Gleason grade per slide and a patient-level train / validation / test partition proposed by the authors.
