Weakly supervised slide classification

The task family behind most slide-level benchmarks: a gigapixel slide is tiled into patches, patch features are aggregated, and only the slide label supervises training. Metastasis detection and cancer subtyping, the applications in the cited paper, are instances of it.[clam-paper]

Prediction target
One label per whole-slide image (or per patient), learned from slide-level labels.
Clinical tasks
Breast lesion subtyping, Gleason grading, Lymph-node metastasis detection, Patient-level pN staging
