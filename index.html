Featured topic

Prostate

Computational work on the prostate centres on needle biopsies and the Gleason grading system. The largest public benchmark is PANDA; most methods treat grading as weakly supervised slide-level classification.

How to read relation labels

Evaluation
A cited source reports evaluating the method on this dataset for the stated task.
Fine-tuning
A cited source reports fine-tuning or training the method on this dataset. Not by itself an evaluation result.
Pretraining
The dataset was part of the pretraining data. Says nothing about downstream performance on it.
Background
Context only: definitions, grading systems or related work. Not a method result.
Same task family
Built for this kind of task. No relation to this dataset is recorded; do not read it as evaluated.
