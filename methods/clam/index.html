CLAM

Clustering-constrained attention MIL. Learns a slide label from bags of patch features and adds an instance-level clustering loss on the most and least attended patches. The repository is also a widely reused WSI tiling and feature-extraction toolkit.

Paper

Lu et al. Data-efficient and weakly supervised computational pathology on whole-slide images. Nat Biomed Eng 5, 555-570 (2021)

Known limitations

  • The other two applications in the paper (renal cell carcinoma and lung cancer subtyping) use TCGA and CPTAC data, which are not catalogued here yet.[clam-paper]
  • Recent versions resize patches to 224 x 224 before feature extraction by default; results can differ from the original paper's setup.[clam-repo]
