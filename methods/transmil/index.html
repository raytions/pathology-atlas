TransMIL

Transformer-based correlated MIL. Instead of treating patches as independent samples, it uses self-attention to model correlations between them, aiming to capture both morphological and spatial information before predicting a slide label.

Paper

Shao et al. TransMIL: Transformer based Correlated Multiple Instance Learning for Whole Slide Image Classification. arXiv 2106.00908 (2021)

Known limitations

  • The README's abstract block describes a different paper (a mixed-supervision Gleason grading model on SICAPv2). Use the NeurIPS / arXiv paper as the reference for what TransMIL is.[transmil-repo]
  • Pinned to old dependencies (Python 3.7, PyTorch 1.x, pytorch-lightning 1.5).[transmil-repo]
