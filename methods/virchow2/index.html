Foundation model (encoder) · 2024

Virchow2

A successor to Virchow trained on about 3.1 million MSKCC whole-slide images with tiles sampled at several magnifications (5x to 40x). Same ViT-H/14 size as Virchow (plus 4 register tokens) but a separate model with different data, weights and licence.

Paper

Zimmermann E. et al. Virchow2: Scaling Self-Supervised Mixed Magnification Models in Pathology (arXiv 2408.00738 v3)
