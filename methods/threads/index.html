Foundation model (encoder) · 2025

THREADS

A slide-level foundation model that turns CONCH v1.5 patch features into one 1024-dimensional slide embedding. Its slide encoder was trained to align each slide with the molecular profile (bulk RNA-seq or targeted DNA panel) of the same tissue, on 47,171 H&E slides from MGH, BWH, TCGA and GTEx.

Paper

Vaidya A., Zhang A., Jaume G. et al. Molecular-driven Foundation Model for Oncologic Pathology (arXiv 2501.16652 v1)

Known limitations

  • Described in a preprint only (arXiv v1, January 2025), and the weights have not been released, so its reported results cannot yet be reproduced with a released model.[threads-preprint]
  • Evaluated only on slide- and patient-level tasks; it reports no results on the patch-level datasets catalogued here (NCT-CRC-HE-100K, CRC-VAL-HE-7K, MHIST, SICAPv2) or on CAMELYON16 / 17.[threads-preprint]
  • Pretraining includes TCGA and GTEx slides, so downstream evaluations on those cohorts can overlap with pretraining data.[threads-preprint]
