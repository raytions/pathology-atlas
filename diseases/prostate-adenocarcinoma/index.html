Disease

Prostate adenocarcinoma

The common form of prostate cancer and the target of Gleason grading. Public computational benchmarks focus on acinar adenocarcinoma in needle biopsies; rarer variants are mostly not assessed separately.

Organ
Prostate
Also called
Prostate cancer; Acinar adenocarcinoma of the prostate
Tasks
Gleason grading, Gleason pattern classification

Datasets

  • PANDA

    Dataset

    The largest public set of prostate biopsy WSIs with grade labels, released for a 2020 Kaggle competition. Each biopsy has an ISUP grade group and Gleason score; many training slides also carry partial masks whose label scheme differs by contributing centre.

    Organ
    Prostate
    Task
    Gleason grading
    Annotation
    Slide label, Region mask
    Access

  • SICAPv2

    Dataset

    Prostate core-needle biopsy patches (512 x 512 at 10x) from 155 slides of 95 patients, each with a pixel-level Gleason grade mask, plus a primary and secondary Gleason grade per slide and a patient-level train / validation / test partition proposed by the authors.
